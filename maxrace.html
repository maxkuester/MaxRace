    Race

    Recording

    MaxRace records the track and the instruments every 2 seconds. When you finish the race it saves the track and builds the performance report: % of polar over time, point of sail summary, leg by leg, verdict.

    ● Recording

    Roundings of the course marks are logged by themselves. Without a course, press "Log mark" at each rounding so the report can split the legs.

    Races

    Every finished race is also saved on this computer in Documents › MaxRace › Races: track (CSV and GPX), report (HTML) and report (PDF).

      Report

      Mark

      Start line by coordinates

      Pin end

      Boat end (committee)

      Type degrees and minutes in the two boxes, tap N/S or E/W to switch hemisphere. Decimal degrees go in the first box alone (45.6542). You can also paste a whole position, like 45°39.250'N 013°44.100'E, into any box. Leave an end empty to keep it as it is (pinged or set before).

      Course

      Sequence

      The order of the marks to sail. The highlighted leg is the active waypoint, used for DTW, BTW, VMC and laylines. A mark can appear more than once (windward, leeward, windward…).

        Marks

          Type degrees and minutes in the two boxes, tap N/S or E/W to switch hemisphere. Decimal degrees go in the first box alone (43.6012). Pasting a whole position into any box works too.

          Mark from bearing and distance

          For marks given as "1.2 nm at 225° from the committee boat". The bearing is from the reference point to the new mark.

          Paste from the sailing instructions

          One mark per line: name; latitude; longitude. Each mark is added to the list and, in that order, to the sequence.

          Settings

          Boats

          Each boat keeps its own polar, manoeuvre values, GPS-to-bow distance and data source (IP and port). Choose the boat at the top left of the screen and everything switches at once. Changes you make in these settings are saved to the boat in use.

            Course

            Marks, their GPS positions and the sequence to sail.

            Data source

            NMEA 0183 from your gateway or instrument system, by IP and port. A browser cannot open TCP or UDP sockets itself, so this goes through the small MaxRace bridge running on a laptop or Raspberry Pi on board (maxrace_bridge.py). Open the dashboard from the bridge address and the bridge field fills itself.

            Signal K WebSocket address on the boat network, for example ws://10.10.10.1:3000/signalk/v1/stream?subscribe=none. Live data works when this dashboard is opened as a file on a device on the boat's network; the hosted claude.ai page cannot reach your boat's server.

            Polar

            The built-in demo polar is a generic placeholder, not your boat’s data. Open your ORC certificate PDF and MaxRace builds the polar from it, or paste or open a polar file. Two formats: Expedition (one line per wind speed: TWS, then pairs of TWA and boat speed, so each wind speed can have its own beat and gybe angle, as in an ORC certificate), or a table (first row a label and the TWS values, then one row per TWA). Tab, semicolon, comma or space separated.

            Manoeuvres at the start

            Used for the luff-up and tack-up arcs and times. The defaults are generic placeholders: replace them with values from your own logs.

            GPS antenna to bow

            Distance from the GPS antenna forward to the bow, in metres. Start line distances and pings are measured from the bow.

            Mark bearing for VMC

            VMC uses the active waypoint from the instruments when they send one. Otherwise enter the bearing to the next mark in degrees true. Leave empty to turn it off.

            Digits

            Typeface for the numbers.

            Damping

            How much the numbers are smoothed. Left is calmer, right reacts faster.
